# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What services do you provide?
We provide high-performance Telegram and Discord automated AI support bots powered by PyTekt and C++ native dispatch.

## 2. What are the operating hours?
Our support team and automated assistant are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## 3. How do refunds work?
We offer a 100% money-back guarantee within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase. To request a refund, contact billing@example.com with your transaction ID.

## 4. How can I reach human support?
You can create a support ticket directly with the `/ticket <issue>` command or email us at support@example.com.

## 5. What payment methods are supported?
We support Telegram Stars, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Credit/Debit cards, and cryptocurrency payments.
